How the work is split

  • Hardware and structures — baseplate, 3D-printed habitat, wiring, controller.
  • AI, software and sensors — crew-state model, adaptation engine, dashboard, this site.
  • Human factors, life support and presentation — zoning, greenhouse, storyboards, trifold, video.

What we have learned so far

  • Snap-fit slots that model at 1.8 mm print too tight; 2.2 mm with a test kit worked better.
  • Fewer, larger print pieces beat many small ones on a school printer schedule.
  • Describe the AI as decision support, never as diagnosis.

Aether is a NASA HUNCH 2026–27 project by students at the Frisco ISD Career and Technical Education Center.

Dimensions, test results and power figures on this site come from our own project documents and experiments and have not been independently verified against NASA sources.
